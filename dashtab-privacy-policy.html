Dashtab Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Dashtab is a privacy-first New Tab extension that replaces Chrome's default New Tab page with a clean, customizable personal dashboard.

Your data belongs to you. Dashtab is designed to operate primarily on your device and does not collect, sell, or share your personal information.

Information Stored

Dashtab stores only the information necessary to provide your personalized dashboard, including:

  • Dashboard layout and widget configuration
  • Theme and appearance preferences
  • Quick links
  • To-do lists
  • Notes
  • Focus goals
  • Pomodoro settings
  • Weather preferences
  • Other dashboard customization settings

This information is stored using Chrome's extension storage and remains on your device unless you remove it.

Bookmarks

If you enable the bookmarks widget, Dashtab reads your Chrome bookmarks to display them on your dashboard for convenient access.

Bookmarks are read locally from your browser and are never transmitted, shared, or analyzed for advertising or profiling.

Browsing History

If you enable the Recent History widget, Dashtab reads your recently visited pages to display them on the dashboard.

Browsing history is used only to populate this optional widget. It is never uploaded, analyzed for behavioral profiling, or shared with third parties.

Top Sites

Dashtab may display Chrome's most frequently visited websites using Chrome's Top Sites API.

This information is read locally and displayed only within your dashboard to improve navigation.

Location

If you enable the Weather widget, Dashtab may request your location permission to retrieve weather information for your area.

Location access is optional and only requested after your consent.

Location data is used solely to obtain current weather conditions and is not stored longer than necessary, shared, or used for tracking.

What We Do Not Collect

Dashtab does not collect or transmit:

  • Personally identifiable information
  • Email addresses
  • Passwords
  • Authentication credentials
  • Financial information
  • Health information
  • Analytics or telemetry
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Keystroke logging
  • Website content
  • Mouse or keyboard activity

Data Transmission

Dashtab does not upload your dashboard data, notes, tasks, bookmarks, browsing history, or other personal information to external servers.

All dashboard functionality is performed locally inside your browser except optional weather requests, which only transmit the minimum location information required to retrieve weather data.

Third-Party Services

Dashtab does not use:

  • Analytics platforms
  • Advertising networks
  • Tracking scripts
  • Cloud databases
  • User profiling services

If the optional Weather widget is enabled, a weather service may be contacted solely to retrieve current weather information for your selected or approved location.

Remote Code

All JavaScript, HTML, CSS, and extension resources are packaged within the extension distributed through the Chrome Web Store.

Dashtab does not download or execute remote JavaScript, WebAssembly, or any other executable code.

Permissions

storage

Stores your dashboard configuration, preferences, notes, tasks, quick links, themes, and other personalization settings locally.

bookmarks

Displays your bookmarks inside the dashboard for quick access.

history

Displays recently visited websites if you enable the Recent History widget.

topSites

Displays Chrome's most frequently visited websites as quick shortcuts.

geolocation

Used only for the optional Weather widget after you grant permission.

Data Retention

Your dashboard data remains stored until you delete it, reset the extension, or uninstall Dashtab.

Children's Privacy

Dashtab is not directed toward children under the age required by applicable law and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Dashtab's functionality changes. The latest version will always be published on this page.

Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the Chrome Web Store listing.
