Privacy Policy for Keyboardly

Effective Date: July 2026

At Keyboardly, we take your privacy incredibly seriously.

1. Local-First Storage

Keyboardly is designed to be fully local-first. All block content, descriptions, color tags, custom JavaScript scripts, options, and usage statistics are stored exclusively inside your local Google Chrome profile using the chrome.storage.local API.

2. No External Server Communications

Keyboardly does not communicate with external servers. No telemetry, statistics, usage analytics, personal identifiers, or block contents are transmitted outside your local browser environment.

3. Principle of Least Privilege

The extension requests only the minimum required permissions to function correctly:

  • storage: Saved local block mapping values.
  • scripting: Runs developer script snippets locally.
  • clipboardRead: Provides selection content fallbacks.
  • activeTab: Targets scripts and content insertion solely to pages you explicitly trigger Keyboardly on.

4. Updates & Policy Changes

We reserve the right to update this policy if required by Google Chrome Web Store guidelines. Any updates will maintain our core promise: we do not collect, monetize, or transmit your personal text data.
