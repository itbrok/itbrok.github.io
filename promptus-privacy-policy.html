Promptus Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Promptus is a browser extension that helps users save, organize, generate, and reuse AI prompts. We believe your prompts belong to you. Promptus is designed with a privacy-first approach and does not collect, sell, or transmit your personal information.

Information We Store

Promptus stores only the information necessary to provide its functionality, including:

  • Your saved prompts
  • Prompt templates
  • Folders and categories
  • Template variables and placeholders
  • User preferences and extension settings

This information is stored using Chrome's extension storage on your browser. It is used solely to provide the features of the extension.

Website Access

Promptus operates only on supported AI websites where users choose to use the extension (such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Copilot, and other supported AI assistants).

The extension injects its interface only to:

  • Display your saved prompt library
  • Generate prompts from templates
  • Insert prompts into the current text editor when requested

Promptus does not monitor your browsing activity outside of these supported websites.

What We Do Not Collect

Promptus does not collect or transmit:

  • Personally identifiable information
  • Email addresses
  • Passwords or authentication credentials
  • Browsing history
  • Location information
  • Financial information
  • Health information
  • Analytics or telemetry data
  • Keystroke logging

Data Transmission

Promptus does not send your prompts or personal data to external servers. Your data remains under your control unless you intentionally copy, export, or paste it elsewhere.

Third-Party Services

Promptus does not use third-party analytics, advertising, tracking services, or remote databases.

Remote Code

Promptus does not download or execute remote JavaScript, WebAssembly, or other executable code. All extension code is packaged with the extension submitted to the Chrome Web Store.

Permissions

storage

Used to save your prompts, folders, templates, settings, and preferences.

scripting

Used to display the Promptus interface and insert prompts into supported AI websites when you choose to use the extension.

Host Permissions

Required only to enable Promptus on supported AI websites. These permissions allow the extension to display its interface and insert prompts where requested. Promptus does not collect browsing history or monitor activity outside of supported sites.

Data Retention

Your stored prompts remain available until you edit or delete them, or uninstall the extension.

Children's Privacy

Promptus is not directed toward children under the age required by applicable law and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the extension's functionality changes. The latest version will always be made available at this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact the developer through the Chrome Web Store listing.
