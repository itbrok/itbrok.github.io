Tibernate Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Tibernate ("the Extension") is designed with privacy as a core principle. The Extension performs all tab management locally within your browser and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal information.

Information We Collect

Tibernate does not collect personal information, browsing history, account information, analytics, or any other user data.

The Extension does not transmit information to external servers.

Local Storage

The Extension stores only its configuration locally using Chrome's storage API. This may include:

  • Automatic suspension timer
  • Excluded websites
  • Excluded pinned tabs
  • Excluded audible tabs
  • User preferences and settings

These settings remain on your own device and are never transmitted outside your browser.

Tab Information

To suspend inactive tabs, Tibernate temporarily accesses information provided by Chrome, such as:

  • Tab URL (only for exclusion matching)
  • Whether a tab is active
  • Whether a tab is pinned
  • Whether audio is playing
  • When the tab was last accessed

This information is processed entirely within your browser and is never stored permanently or transmitted anywhere.

Data Sharing

Tibernate does not:

  • Sell user data
  • Share user data with third parties
  • Transfer user data to external services
  • Use analytics or tracking services
  • Use advertising SDKs
  • Collect telemetry

Remote Code

All code required by the Extension is packaged with the extension itself. Tibernate does not download or execute remote JavaScript, WebAssembly, or other executable code.

Security

Because all processing occurs locally, your browser data remains on your device. The Extension does not maintain servers or cloud infrastructure.

Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy changes, the updated version will be published alongside future releases of the Extension.

Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the Chrome Web Store listing or the project's official repository.
